# Privacy policy

**Duo** — `com.therealsoftware.duo`
Last updated: 13 September 2026

## The short version

Duo collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no
server. Your data never reaches us, because there is nowhere for it to go.

## What the app does with your data

**Nothing leaves your two phones.** Duo connects one phone to another over your
own WiFi network. Everything it sends travels between those two devices and
stops there.

**The video you choose.** When you pick a video, the app reads that file and
serves it to the other phone over your local network. The file is not copied
anywhere else, not uploaded anywhere, and not kept after you close the app. The
other phone receives it only while the two are connected.

**The web address you type.** In web mode, your phone loads that page directly
from the website, exactly as a browser would. That website will see your IP
address and anything else a normal visit reveals. Duo does not sit in the middle
and cannot see or keep what you load.

## What the app stores

Duo stores nothing on your device beyond what the Android system keeps for any
running app. Closing the app ends the session. There is no history, no saved
list of the videos you played, and no saved list of the addresses you opened.

## What the app asks for

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET` | To reach the other phone over WiFi, and to load a page in web mode |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | To find the other phone on the network |

Duo asks for no storage permission. The video you pick comes through Android's
own photo picker, which hands the app that one file and nothing else.

## Children

Duo is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

## Third-party services

None. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising
library and no social login. The libraries it does contain are listed in
[NOTICE](NOTICE), and none of them collect data for us.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be committed here and the
date above will move. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect it to.
